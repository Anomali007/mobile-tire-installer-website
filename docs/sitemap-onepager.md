# Don’s Mobile Tire Service — One-Page Sitemap

## Primary Goal
Convert visitors into bookings (or calls/texts) fast, while building trust for first-time customers and credibility for financing/partners.

---

## Core Pages (MVP Website)

### 1) Home
**Purpose:** Fast booking intent + trust
**Primary CTA:** Book Now
**Secondary CTA:** Call/Text

Sections:
- Hero: “Mobile Tire Service — We Come To You” + CTA
- Services grid (3–6)
- How it works (4 steps)
- Coverage area snapshot
- Pricing promise / deposit policy
- Reviews / proof (photos)
- Fleet / commercial teaser
- FAQ preview
- Final CTA + contact info

---

### 2) Book Now
**Purpose:** Single focused conversion page that launches the booking flow (or simple form if MVP)
Contains:
- Quick service selector (New Tires / Rotate & Balance / TPMS / Flat Repair)
- Address + preferred time window
- “Deposit to book” explanation
- Call/Text fallback

---

### 3) Services (Overview)
**Purpose:** Explain what you do + route people into booking
Includes service tiles linking to detail pages.

**Service detail pages (recommended for SEO + clarity):**
- Mobile Tire Install (New Tires)
- Rotate & Balance
- TPMS Service
- Flat Repair / Roadside (if offered)
- Used Tires (if offered)
Each detail page includes: what’s included, what to expect, FAQs, pricing notes, Book CTA.

---

### 4) Coverage Area
**Purpose:** Reduce unqualified leads + build confidence
Includes:
- Map/list of cities + ZIPs served
- Hours + typical booking windows
- “Not sure?” text us your address

---

### 5) Pricing & Fees
**Purpose:** Trust + fewer surprises
Includes:
- Transparent fee structure (mobile service fee, mount/balance, disposal, TPMS, etc.)
- Deposit policy + cancellation policy
- Example receipt/line items

---

### 6) Fleet / Commercial
**Purpose:** Land recurring revenue + “fundable” look
Includes:
- Who it’s for (property managers, delivery fleets, dealerships)
- Benefits (priority windows, invoicing, centralized scheduling)
- CTA: Request Fleet Pricing

---

### 7) Reviews / Proof
**Purpose:** Credibility
Includes:
- Google reviews embed (or curated testimonials)
- Before/after photos
- “Recent installs” gallery (optional)

---

### 8) About
**Purpose:** Human trust + legitimacy
Includes:
- Don’s story + mission
- Safety/quality standards
- Photos of truck/tools/team

---

### 9) FAQ
**Purpose:** Reduce friction + increase conversion
Topics:
- Service area
- Deposit / refunds / reschedules
- Do I need to be present?
- Weather
- Tire sourcing (customer provides vs you supply)
- Duration and what’s required (lug key, wheel lock, etc.)

---

### 10) Contact
**Purpose:** Easy conversion fallback
Includes:
- Click-to-call + click-to-text
- Form
- Hours + coverage reminder

---

## Utility Pages
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service
- Cancellation / Refund Policy
